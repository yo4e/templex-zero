# Post-Study-008-NO-GO Selection Threshold

_Date: 2026-07-30 (Asia/Tokyo)_  
_Status: **Frozen before candidate research, availability preflight, scoring, or selection**_

## Purpose

This document fixes the decision rule for the first portfolio assessment after the Study 008 activation NO-GO. The baseline is to remain inactive. A new direction must add information that Studies 001–007 and the rejected Study 008 activation do not already supply.

Study 008 adds a hard correction to project selection: a paper, README, registry entry, or historical public link is not evidence that an indispensable external artifact is currently retrievable, safely parseable, licensed for the intended use, and pinnable by exact bytes.

## Order of operations

The portfolio decision must follow this order:

1. commit this threshold;
2. identify materially distinct candidate directions without assigning scores;
3. for every indispensable external artifact, perform the outcome-blind referent-availability preflight below;
4. reject any direction that fails the preflight before scoring;
5. score only surviving directions;
6. compare them with inactivity;
7. freeze at most one inactive proposal or remain inactive.

Candidate-specific outcome values, correlations, rankings, labels, verdict distributions, benchmark scores, or protected relationships may not be inspected during availability preflight.

## Outcome-blind referent-availability preflight

For every external artifact required to define the denominator or primary evidence, all of the following must be established from an official source before the direction may be scored:

1. **Official identity** — an official repository, standards body, publisher, institution, or author-controlled location can be identified and pinned to a commit, release, or version.
2. **Byte accessibility** — the exact required file is currently retrievable without credentials, new terms acceptance, payment, private access, or a third-party mirror.
3. **Safe format** — the required artifact is available in a format that can be inspected without arbitrary-code execution, unsafe deserialization, hostile document execution, or model inference.
4. **License visibility** — the license or explicit use terms for the exact artifact are visible enough to support bounded repository research. A repository software license does not automatically license separately hosted data.
5. **High-level schema identity** — file names, content type, approximate byte scale, record form, identifiers, and required field families can be verified without computing protected relationships or outcome summaries.
6. **Denominator plausibility** — the advertised denominator can be checked structurally from metadata or schema without dropping records, substituting mirrors, reconstructing missing fields, or observing protected outcomes.
7. **Temporal sufficiency** — there is no unresolved official evidence that the exact artifact is unavailable, withdrawn, superseded without identity, or incomplete for the proposed use.

Failure of any item is a hard feasibility failure. The direction is not scored and cannot displace inactivity in this cycle.

Availability preflight may download or inspect only the minimum bytes needed to establish these conditions. It may not retain or analyze a formal corpus, compute scientific statistics, inspect protected labels in aggregate, or begin implementation.

## Scored criteria

Each direction that passes availability preflight is scored from 0 to 5 on ten criteria:

1. **Information value** — likely to change a substantive or methodological belief rather than merely repair an authored instrument or repeat a known result.
2. **External referent strength** — primary evidence is meaningfully independent of TEMPLEX/0 and is not selected because it is easy to formalize.
3. **Epistemic diversification** — changes the object, evidence type, and failure surface relative to Studies 001–007.
4. **Value exposure beyond conformance** — confronts interpretation, usefulness, judgment, tradeoffs, uncertainty, or another quality not reducible to exact agreement counts.
5. **Falsifiability and auditability** — supports explicit negative, partial, setup-failure, operationally incomplete, or unresolved outcomes.
6. **Observational validity discipline** — preserves materially distinct populations, dimensions, operation families, uncertainty sources, or label types rather than collapsing them without evidence.
7. **Execution feasibility and authority fit** — can be completed using available tools and permissions, with no hidden dependency on unavailable compute, continuous operation, external contact, or human recruitment.
8. **Stopping clarity** — has fixed denominators, resource caps, invalidation rules, and a credible maximum-four-cycle closure plan.
9. **Reusable contribution** — can leave an independently useful dataset projection, method, instrument, analysis, or decision record even if its hypotheses fail.
10. **Resistance to self-confirmation** — TEMPLEX/0 does not control both the primary evidence and the rule that would make the result positive.

Scores are explicit research judgments, not measurements.

## Selection threshold

A surviving direction may displace inactivity only if all of the following hold:

- total score is at least **43 of 50**;
- no criterion is below **4**;
- epistemic diversification is **5**;
- observational validity discipline is **5**;
- execution feasibility and authority fit is **5**;
- the primary evidence is not another exact-conformance matrix of the general form used in Studies 004–007;
- the direction is not primarily a repaired rerun of Study 007, a repaired activation of Study 008, or another authored expectation;
- the main question remains meaningful if every hypothesis is unsupported;
- the study can close within **four approval cycles** from activation through report;
- no required artifact depends on a mirror, guessed identity, reconstruction of unavailable fields, source-corpus reacquisition, unsafe format, non-pinned model inference, or reduced denominator;
- the work requires no unsolicited contact, third-party repository write, private data, spending, contracts, permission changes, hostile-input execution, or new publication channel.

A direction that meets the numeric total but fails any hard gate is not selected.

## Presumptions against repetition

The following are presumed non-selectable unless they present extraordinary contrary evidence:

- another standards, parser, filesystem, transaction, archive, timezone, or finite-state conformance matrix;
- rerunning Study 007 after correcting the known `RESTRICT` expectation;
- replacing the unavailable SummEval scored file with a mirror or a recomputed substitute;
- internal reproducibility infrastructure promoted into a study without an external research question;
- self-evaluation or project-selection calibration whose labels are authored entirely by TEMPLEX/0;
- a small replication of a recent large audit that does not change the evidence or decision problem.

## Human-judgment condition

A direction may use existing released human judgments when official artifact availability passes the preflight and the study preserves population, task, rubric, and uncertainty boundaries.

New human observation or recruitment is non-selectable in this cycle unless the task is methodologically indispensable, bounded, authorized, fully precommitted, and failure to obtain it can remain an unresolved outcome. No such recruitment is authorized by ordinary approval.

## Authorized result of this cycle

This cycle may:

- inspect Studies 001–007, the Study 008 proposal and NO-GO decision, governance, self-model, failure modes, issues, and recent commits;
- perform minimum outcome-blind official-artifact availability preflights;
- compare materially distinct directions plus inactivity;
- freeze at most one inactive proposal;
- update decision, state, restart, failure-mode or self records when evidence supports it, and human-intervention records.

It may not activate a study, open an active-study issue, retain or analyze a formal corpus, implement an instrument, compute protected statistics, inspect outcome distributions, contact outsiders, or alter a closed or rejected proposal.
