# Post-Study-009-NO-GO Selection Threshold

_Date: 2026-07-30 (Asia/Tokyo)_  
_Status: **Frozen before candidate research, materialization rehearsal, scoring, or selection**_

## Purpose

This document fixes the decision rule for the first portfolio assessment after the Study 009 activation NO-GO. The baseline is to remain inactive. A new direction must add information that Studies 001–007 and the rejected Study 008 and Study 009 activations do not already supply.

Studies 008 and 009 establish two separate failures. A documented official artifact may no longer be retrievable, and a visible official commit, path, or Git object may still be unusable in the actual execution environment. Feasibility therefore requires an end-to-end rehearsal, not metadata confidence.

Materializability is necessary but not sufficient. A direction does not gain research value merely because its inputs are local, small, textual, or easy to hash.

## Order of operations

The portfolio decision must follow this order:

1. commit this threshold;
2. identify materially distinct directions without assigning scores;
3. identify every indispensable artifact, environment, human operation, permission, and execution path for each direction;
4. perform the outcome-blind execution-path rehearsal below;
5. reject every direction that fails any hard rehearsal condition before scoring;
6. score only surviving directions;
7. compare survivors with inactivity;
8. freeze at most one inactive proposal or remain inactive.

No candidate-specific scientific outcome, relationship, distribution, ranking, label rate, benchmark result, hypothesis predicate, or selectively favorable example may be inspected during rehearsal.

## Outcome-blind end-to-end execution-path rehearsal

Every indispensable input or environment must pass the applicable conditions before the direction may be scored.

### External byte artifacts

For each indispensable external artifact:

1. **Official identity** — identify an official or author-controlled repository, publisher, institution, release, or version.
2. **Authorized acquisition** — acquire the exact bytes through an available authorized path without credentials, payment, new terms acceptance, third-party mirrors, manual corpus reconstruction, or external human action.
3. **Untransformed materialization** — save the acquired bytes locally without text reserialization, copy-and-paste reconstruction, format conversion, decompression in place of the original object, or connector rendering as the sole retained representation.
4. **Independent identity verification** — compute byte length and SHA-256 locally and, when applicable, verify the Git-blob, release digest, signature, or another official identity independently from the acquisition metadata.
5. **Safe parser opening** — open the exact object with the intended bounded parser without arbitrary-code execution, unsafe deserialization, hostile document execution, model inference, subprocess evaluators, or network-dependent parsing.
6. **Terms and provenance visibility** — confirm license or use terms for the exact artifact and preserve required notices.
7. **Schema and denominator plausibility** — verify only the minimum structural facts needed to establish that the proposed population can be frozen without mirrors, guessed fields, repaired identifiers, reduced denominators, or outcome inspection.
8. **Repeatable path** — the same authorized acquisition and parser path must be credibly available for activation and clean reproduction.

A temporary rehearsal copy may be deleted after verification and may not be committed as a formal corpus in this portfolio cycle. Failure of any item is a hard feasibility failure.

### Local or installed targets

A direction relying on a local runtime, package, filesystem, executable, database, or other installed target must preflight:

1. exact version, package, source, and environment identity;
2. read and execution permissions;
3. availability of every required command, library, path, and isolation boundary;
4. one harmless capability check using non-protected synthetic input;
5. a credible exact reconstruction path for the final reproduction cycle.

Local availability does not exempt a direction from epistemic-diversification, value, or anti-repetition requirements.

### Existing repository artifacts

A direction using TEMPLEX/0's own records must identify an external research claim that is not defined entirely by TEMPLEX/0-authored labels or success criteria. Self-analysis, process calibration, or infrastructure work alone cannot displace inactivity.

### Human operations or judgments

A direction whose protected denominator requires new human recruitment, repeated human availability, specialized expertise, or subjective labels does not pass under ordinary approval. A small human contribution may survive only when it is methodologically indispensable, fully precommitted, explicitly authorized at the proper intervention level, and absence can remain an unresolved result rather than forcing replacement.

## Scored criteria

Each direction that passes the rehearsal is scored from 0 to 5 on ten criteria:

1. **Information value** — likely to change a substantive or methodological belief rather than merely repair an instrument, demonstrate access, or repeat a known result.
2. **External referent strength** — primary evidence or the decision problem is meaningfully independent of TEMPLEX/0.
3. **Epistemic diversification** — changes the object, evidence type, and failure surface relative to Studies 001–007 and rejected Studies 008–009.
4. **Value exposure beyond conformance** — confronts interpretation, usefulness, decision tradeoffs, uncertainty, human consequences, or another quality not reducible to exact agreement counts.
5. **Falsifiability and auditability** — supports explicit positive, negative, partial, setup-failure, operationally incomplete, or unresolved outcomes.
6. **Observational validity discipline** — preserves materially distinct populations, dimensions, operation families, time horizons, uncertainty sources, or judgment types.
7. **Execution-path confidence** — the actual authorized acquisition, materialization, parser, environment, and reproduction paths have passed rehearsal without substitution.
8. **Stopping clarity** — has fixed denominators, resource caps, invalidation rules, and a credible maximum-four-cycle closure plan.
9. **Reusable contribution** — can leave an independently useful artifact, method, analysis, or decision record even if all hypotheses fail.
10. **Resistance to self-confirmation** — TEMPLEX/0 does not control both the primary evidence and the rule that would make the result positive.

Scores are explicit research judgments, not measurements.

## Selection threshold

A surviving direction may displace inactivity only if all of the following hold:

- total score is at least **44 of 50**;
- no criterion is below **4**;
- epistemic diversification is **5**;
- value exposure beyond conformance is **5**;
- observational validity discipline is **5**;
- execution-path confidence is **5**;
- the primary evidence is not another exact-conformance matrix of the general form used in Studies 004–007;
- the direction is not primarily a repaired activation of Study 008 or Study 009, a repaired Study 007 oracle, or another authored expectation;
- the question remains meaningful if every hypothesis is unsupported;
- full activation through closure is credible within **four approval cycles**;
- no indispensable dependency requires a different execution environment, later tool availability, human file transfer, mirror, manually reconstructed corpus, guessed identity, non-pinned model inference, hidden continuous service, or reduced denominator;
- the work requires no unsolicited contact, third-party repository write, private data, spending, contract, permission change, hostile-input execution, security advice, or new publication channel.

A direction that meets the numeric total but fails a hard condition is not selected.

## Presumptions against low-value recovery

The following are presumed non-selectable unless extraordinary contrary evidence is established before scoring:

- repairing the Study 008 or Study 009 source path and immediately rerunning the same proposal;
- selecting a corpus chiefly because the connector can return it as text;
- selecting a local standard-library or package target chiefly to avoid external acquisition;
- another standards, parser, filesystem, transaction, archive, timezone, or finite-state conformance matrix;
- internal reproducibility or download infrastructure promoted into a study without an external substantive question;
- self-evaluation or selection calibration whose labels are authored entirely by TEMPLEX/0;
- repeating aggregate-versus-exact false-reassurance designs without a materially different population, decision, and evidence type.
