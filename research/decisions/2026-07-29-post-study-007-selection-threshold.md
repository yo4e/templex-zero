# Post-Study-007 Selection Threshold

_Date: 2026-07-29 (Asia/Tokyo)_  
_Status: **Frozen before candidate research, scoring, or selection**_

## Purpose

This document fixes the decision rule for the first portfolio assessment after Study 007. The baseline is to remain inactive. Activity must displace inactivity by offering information that the seven completed studies do not already supply.

Study 007 adds a specific warning: semantic similarity is not evidence of exact observational equivalence. A candidate must justify the granularity of every frozen projection and must not earn value merely by repairing an expectation authored by TEMPLEX/0.

## Scored criteria

Each active direction is scored from 0 to 5 on ten criteria:

1. **Information value** — likely to change a substantive or methodological belief rather than merely complete or repair a known instrument.
2. **External referent strength** — anchored in evidence, people, artifacts, environments, or specifications not authored by TEMPLEX/0.
3. **Epistemic diversification** — changes the object, evidence type, and failure surface relative to Studies 001–007.
4. **Value exposure beyond conformance** — confronts usefulness, interpretation, judgment, or another quality not reducible to exact agreement counts alone.
5. **Falsifiability and auditability** — admits clear negative, partial, setup-failure, or unresolved outcomes with inspectable evidence.
6. **Observational validity discipline** — can freeze projections at a granularity supported by pre-outcome evidence, with materially distinct operation or judgment families separately gated.
7. **Feasibility and authority fit** — can be performed with available tools, permissions, bounded human assistance if explicitly required, and no hidden dependency on unavailable execution.
8. **Stopping clarity** — has bounded denominators, resource caps, invalidation rules, and a credible four-cycle closure plan.
9. **Reusable contribution** — can leave an independently useful artifact, corpus, method, instrument, or analysis even if the main hypothesis fails.
10. **Resistance to self-confirmation** — a positive result is not made easy by TEMPLEX/0 defining both the mechanism and the success conditions.

Scores are explicit research judgments, not measurements.

## Selection threshold

A direction may displace inactivity only if all of the following hold:

- total score is at least **42 of 50**;
- no criterion is below **4**;
- epistemic diversification is **5**;
- observational validity discipline is **5**;
- the primary evidence is not another exact-conformance matrix of the same general form as Studies 004–007;
- the direction is not primarily a repaired rerun of Study 007 or another authored expectation;
- a credible result can be negative, partial, operationally incomplete, or unresolved without changing the denominator or evaluation rule after outcome inspection;
- the study can close within **four approval cycles** from activation through report;
- this portfolio cycle can freeze at most one inactive proposal without activation, implementation, corpus execution, protected outcome inspection, or external action;
- the work requires no unsolicited contact, third-party repository operation, private data, spending, terms acceptance, permission change, hostile-input execution, or new publication channel.

A direction that meets the numeric total but fails any floor or hard gate is not selected.

## Exact-conformance presumption

A further standards-, API-, parser-, filesystem-, transaction-, or finite-state-conformance study is presumed **non-selectable** unless it demonstrates all three:

1. a materially different evidence type rather than a new corpus under the same method;
2. value beyond correctness counts or deterministic reproduction;
3. an explicit defense against observational category collapse covering every materially distinct projected family before protected execution.

Repairing Study 007's `RESTRICT` error mapping is useful maintenance but does not by itself constitute new research information.

## Human-judgment condition

A direction may use bounded human observation only when:

- the human contribution is methodologically necessary rather than decorative;
- the task, rubric, number of judgments, blinding or ordering rules, and stopping rule can be frozen before responses;
- the contribution will be logged at its actual A-level;
- the study does not present human judgment as objective ground truth beyond the bounded task.

Dependence on human judgment lowers feasibility unless the required operation is small, explicit, and replaceable by an unresolved outcome if unavailable.

## Authorized result of this cycle

This cycle may:

- inspect all seven closed studies and current public referents;
- compare multiple materially distinct directions plus inactivity;
- select at most one direction that clears this threshold;
- freeze at most one inactive proposal;
- update decision, state, restart, self-model, and intervention records.

It may not activate a study, open an active-study issue, implement an instrument, acquire or execute a formal corpus, inspect protected outcomes, contact outsiders, or alter a closed-study expectation.
