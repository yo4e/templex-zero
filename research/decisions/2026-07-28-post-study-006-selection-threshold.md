# Post-Study-006 Selection Threshold

_Date: 2026-07-28 (Asia/Tokyo)_  
_Status: **Frozen before candidate scoring or selection**_

## Purpose

This document fixes the decision rule for the first portfolio assessment after Study 006. It is intentionally committed before candidate scores, a selected direction, or a new proposal.

The baseline is to remain inactive. Activity must displace that baseline rather than be presumed valuable.

## Scored criteria

Each active direction is scored from 0 to 5 on eight criteria:

1. **Information value** — likely to change TEMPLEX/0's substantive or methodological beliefs rather than merely confirm an implementation.
2. **Falsifiability and auditability** — admits exact negative, partial, or setup-failure outcomes with independently inspectable evidence.
3. **External referent strength** — rests on a versioned specification, implementation contract, dataset, or operational boundary not authored by TEMPLEX/0.
4. **Epistemic diversification** — changes the object, evidence type, and failure surface relative to Studies 001–006.
5. **Feasibility** — can be safely represented and completed using currently available tools, permissions, runtime, and repository-only authority.
6. **Stopping clarity** — has bounded denominators, resource caps, invalidation conditions, and a credible four-cycle closure plan.
7. **Reusable contribution** — can leave an independently useful instrument, corpus, oracle, or analysis even if the hypotheses fail.
8. **Resistance to self-confirmation** — a positive result is not made easy by TEMPLEX/0 authoring both the success conditions and the tested mechanism.

Scores are explicit research judgments, not empirical measurements.

## Selection threshold

A direction may displace inactivity only if all of the following hold:

- total score is at least **34 of 40**;
- no criterion is below **4**;
- the external referent is not authored by TEMPLEX/0;
- the primary failure surface is materially different from the value-projection conformance pattern of Study 005 and the filesystem-boundary conformance pattern of Study 006;
- a plausible result can be negative, partial, or operationally incomplete without changing the denominator or method after outcome inspection;
- the study can close within **four approval cycles** from activation through report;
- this portfolio cycle can freeze the proposal without activation, implementation, corpus execution, or protected outcome inspection;
- the work requires no external contact, third-party repository operation, untrusted-input execution, private data, spending, terms acceptance, permission change, or new publication channel.

A direction that meets the numeric total but fails any floor or hard gate is not selected.

## Anti-repetition rule

A further standards-conformance study is not automatically disqualified, but it must introduce a qualitatively new state model or operational failure mode and leave a reusable artifact beyond a list of passing vectors. Merely replacing one mature standard library API with another is insufficient.

## Authorized result of this cycle

This cycle may:

- compare multiple substantively distinct directions plus inactivity;
- select at most one direction that clears this threshold;
- freeze at most one inactive proposal;
- update decision, state, restart, self-model, and intervention records.

It may not activate a study, open an active-study issue, implement an instrument, acquire a formal corpus, run a protected experiment, inspect protected outcomes, or contact anyone outside the repository.
