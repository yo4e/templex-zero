## 4. Public referents inspected

### 4.1 SummEval dimension-specific proxy reliability

The official SummEval paper and repository report:

- 100 source articles;
- summaries from 16 systems, for 1,600 summary records;
- five crowd and three expert annotations per summary;
- four separate human-evaluation dimensions: coherence, consistency, fluency, and relevance;
- released model outputs, human annotations, and automatic metric scores;
- an MIT-licensed public repository.

Primary referents:

- `https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.24/`
- `https://github.com/Yale-LILY/SummEval`

The direction can use the released annotation JSONL and metric fields without reconstructing the CNN/DailyMail source articles, generating new summaries, invoking external models, loading unsafe serialized objects, or contacting human subjects. Its primary question is not which metric wins a leaderboard. It is whether aggregation across dimensions or populations creates false reassurance that disappears when the judgments are kept separate.

Related work already establishes that summarization metric conclusions can depend on aggregation level, system differences, quality criteria, and human protocol. This reduces novelty but strengthens the external basis and makes the study a deliberate test of TEMPLEX/0's own proxy preference rather than a claim to discover the field's problem first.

Additional referents inspected:

- `https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.751/` — REALSumm and released human judgments;
- `https://aclanthology.org/2022.naacl-main.442/` — system-level correlation can misrepresent realistic near-tie use;
- `https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.924/` — multi-criterion correlations can be spurious;
- `https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.635/` — human-evaluation protocols are themselves frequently underreported.

## 6. Why SummEval passes

### 6.1 It changes the evidence type

The primary referent is not an implementation specification or synthetic oracle. It is a released set of human judgments over generated language. The analysis remains quantitative and auditable, but the values being compared were not authored by TEMPLEX/0.

### 6.2 It exposes formal proxies to multiple human dimensions

The study does not presume that one number represents summary quality. Coherence, consistency, fluency, and relevance remain separate, as do expert and crowd populations. Any aggregate is treated as a suspect proxy to be tested, not as ground truth.

### 6.3 It carries forward the strongest previous lesson without repeating the same method

Studies 001 and 002 showed that aggregate rates can hide local structural failure. Study 007 showed that category collapse can invalidate an exact oracle. SummEval allows those lessons to be tested against external human judgments through correlations, rankings, uncertainty, and disagreement cases rather than another exact pass/fail corpus.

### 6.4 It is bounded and safely reproducible

The official release is finite and public. A later activation can pin one upstream commit and one safe text annotation file, freeze the exact metric inventory and direction metadata before reading values, implement statistics with no network or model inference, and close within four cycles.

### 6.5 A negative or unresolved result remains informative

The study may find that dimension-specific false reassurance is rare, that expert and crowd rankings are stable, that uncertainty is too large for a conclusion, or that the upstream file cannot support the promised separation. None requires changing the denominator or inventing a positive result.

## 7. Risks and controls

- **Known literature:** the field already knows automatic metrics and human judgments can disagree. The study claims no novelty and asks a narrower question about category collapse and false reassurance under one pinned release.
- **Human ratings are not objective truth:** expert and crowd results remain separate; disagreement between them is evidence, not noise to average away.
- **Metric multiplicity:** the exact eligible metric list and higher/lower direction must be frozen from upstream documentation before values are inspected. No post-result metric selection.
- **Aggregation choice:** every dimension and population is primary. Composite rankings are objects under criticism and cannot replace the separate results.
- **Statistical instability:** documents, not individual summary rows, are the bootstrap unit. Confidence intervals and tied ranks must be preserved.
- **Dataset age and domain:** claims remain limited to the pinned SummEval CNN/DailyMail-era outputs and annotation protocol.
- **Unsafe or unavailable data:** activation is NO-GO if the required data is not available as safely parseable text under a clear license or if exact identities cannot be pinned.
- **Source-article absence:** the study uses released human judgments and released metric scores only. It does not reconstruct source articles or independently certify the correctness of the human consistency or relevance labels.
