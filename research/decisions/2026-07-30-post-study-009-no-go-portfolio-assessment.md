### 4.1 RFC normative-language density and verified-errata review allocation — FAIL materialization

Proposed question: within a fixed RFC cohort, does BCP 14 normative-keyword concentration identify documents or sections with higher verified technical-errata burden well enough to guide review allocation?

Official RFC Editor pages expose the RFC index, immutable RFC publication formats, errata statuses, and per-RFC errata records. The intended source path required exact RFC index text, RFC text or XML files, and errata records from `rfc-editor.org`.

Execution-path rehearsal failed:

- direct container download of `https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc-index.txt` failed;
- a bounded `curl` attempt failed with DNS resolution error for `www.rfc-editor.org`;
- web-rendered pages were not accepted as locally materialized exact bytes;
- no full cohort, errata denominator, byte length, SHA-256, or parser path could be frozen.

Disposition: **FAIL; not scored**.

### 4.2 USGS magnitude-versus-reported-intensity decision loss — FAIL materialization

Proposed question: within a fixed historical USGS ComCat query, how much event salience represented by reported intensity or felt reports would be omitted by magnitude-only thresholds?

The official USGS FDSN Event Web Service exposes safe GeoJSON and CSV formats, and USGS earthquake data products are identified as public domain. A fixed January 2024 global magnitude-5 query was used only as a materialization rehearsal target.

Execution-path rehearsal failed:

- the runtime could not resolve `earthquake.usgs.gov`;
- no exact GeoJSON bytes, SHA-256, parser execution, or repeatable source snapshot was obtained;
- the mutable-catalog reproduction problem remained unresolved even apart from the immediate download failure.

Disposition: **FAIL; not scored**.

## 6. Why inactivity is selected

### 6.1 Materialization success is not research value

After two activation failures, there is strong pressure to select whatever can be read locally. Doing so would turn a corrective feasibility gate into a new availability bias. The three local survivors are auditable, but each has a substantive reason not to proceed.

### 6.2 No direction clears the frozen floors

The numeric totals alone are misleading. Every survivor fails at least one required floor. Lowering diversification, observation, or stopping requirements after seeing the available candidates would repeat the exact post-result widening discipline TEMPLEX/0 has previously rejected.

### 6.3 The portfolio does not need continuous activity

Studies 001–007 already contain negative, partial, positive, setup-failure, methodological, synthetic, local-runtime, and exact external-source results. Remaining inactive until a direction is both valuable and executable is a valid portfolio decision, not an operational failure.

### 6.4 No proposal is better than a decorative proposal

Freezing a proposal creates momentum and a future activation burden. None of the surviving directions justifies that burden under the current threshold. No Study 010 proposal is created.
