## 2. Unit of re-entry evidence

A re-entry claim must identify one concrete **opportunity record**. An opportunity record is not a research proposal. It is a bounded statement of:

1. what materially changed since the post-Study-009 portfolio decision;
2. what externally governed decision problem or evidence opportunity the change exposes;
3. which authorized acquisition, execution, or human-evidence path is now available;
4. why the change is not merely easier access to an already rejected or low-value direction;
5. where the change is evidenced in the repository, connected tool response, runtime, or explicit human authorization.

Vague possibility, remembered availability, a URL, a model suggestion, an exact `承認`, or the passage of time is not an opportunity record.

## 3. Two-axis re-entry rule

Portfolio candidate research may reopen only when one opportunity record passes both Axis S and Axis E. Evidence may come from one event or a tightly linked set of events, but the substantive and execution claims must refer to the same opportunity.

### Axis S — Substantive change

At least one of the following must be established:

- **S1 — New decision problem:** a concrete externally meaningful decision or uncertainty not already answered by Studies 001–009 and not defined only by TEMPLEX/0-authored labels or success criteria.
- **S2 — New externally governed referent:** an independently governed primary referent, population, event stream, standard, archive, environment, or institution creates a materially different evidence type or decision surface from the existing portfolio.
- **S3 — Authorized human-evidence opportunity:** explicit consent, access, and authority now exist for a bounded human judgment or observation that is methodologically indispensable and cannot be replaced by TEMPLEX/0 or model-authored labels.

Axis S fails when the claimed novelty is only a repaired Study 008 or Study 009 source path, another exact-conformance matrix, internal process calibration, infrastructure work, or a new framing of an already examined object without a materially different decision.

### Axis E — Executable evidence path

At least one of the following must be established for the same opportunity:

- **E1 — Demonstrated capability change:** a previously unavailable acquisition or handoff class now passes a fresh frozen harmless probe through exact local bytes, local byte length and SHA-256, bounded safe-parser opening, and credible same-path reproduction.
- **E2 — Complete local external referent:** the exact externally governed source already exists through a complete authorized local path with provenance, terms, source identity, local hashing, bounded parser access, and a credible reconstruction or reproduction path.
- **E3 — Authorized human-evidence path:** the people, consent, task boundary, timing, recording method, and absence handling are explicitly authorized and can be frozen without recruitment by implication or repeated unbounded availability.

Metadata visibility, web rendering, connector response content, Git blob identity, base64 presentation, a user-offered description without the source object, or an untested future tool capability does not satisfy Axis E.

## 4. Joint decision rule

The later portfolio may reopen only when:

1. one opportunity record passes at least one S condition;
2. the same opportunity passes at least one E condition;
3. no hard exclusion below applies;
4. the opportunity plausibly permits a candidate comparison cycle without inspecting protected scientific outcomes;
5. reopening means permission to research and compare candidates only, not automatic proposal selection or activation.

A capability change alone does not reopen the portfolio. A valuable question without an executable evidence path does not reopen the portfolio. A locally available object without a materially new decision problem does not reopen the portfolio.

## 5. Hard exclusions

Re-entry is prohibited when the main justification is any of:

- the laboratory has been inactive for a while;
- another exact `承認` was received;
- continuous activity would look more autonomous or productive;
- a rejected Study 008 or Study 009 proposal may now be repairable;
- a local object is convenient to hash or parse;
- infrastructure maintenance is being promoted into a scientific study;
- the proposed question is authored chiefly to fit the available bytes;
- the evidence path requires a mirror, manual corpus reconstruction, guessed identity, reduced denominator, hidden continuous service, new terms acceptance, spending, credentials, or unauthorized human recruitment;
- the opportunity merely repeats the object, evidence type, and failure surface of Studies 001–009 without a materially new decision.
